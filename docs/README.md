# OctoAcme Project Management Process Docs

## Overview

This collection of documents defines how OctoAcme runs projects across initiation, planning, execution, delivery, and learning. Our approach is designed to help cross-functional teams align on outcomes, deliver value iteratively, and maintain clear accountability throughout the project lifecycle.

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model grounded in five core principles:

- Customer-first: prioritize value, usability, and business impact
- Iterative delivery: break work into small, testable increments
- Clear ownership: assign accountable roles and responsibilities
- Data-informed decisions: use metrics, feedback, and evidence to guide work
- Psychological safety: encourage transparency, learning, and constructive feedback

## Project Management Lifecycle

OctoAcme’s project lifecycle follows a simple, repeatable flow:

1. Initiation — define the problem, stakeholders, outcomes, and readiness for planning
2. Planning — align scope, milestones, backlog, dependencies, and ownership
3. Execution — deliver work in increments, monitor risk, and track progress
4. Release — validate quality, deploy carefully, and communicate status
5. Close & retrospective — capture learning and identify improvements

## Core Workflows at a Glance

### Delivery workflow
- Use a project board with stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done
- Keep pull requests small and focused when possible
- Include issue links and acceptance criteria in PR descriptions
- Run automated tests, linting, and security checks before merge
- Require approval before merging to maintain quality and accountability

### Team rhythm
- Daily standups for progress, blockers, and dependencies
- Weekly PM + PdM sync or delivery update
- Demo/review at milestone or sprint boundaries
- Retrospective after sprints, releases, or major milestones

### Risk and escalation
- Level 1: team triage during standups
- Level 2: PM escalates to Product Lead and dependent teams
- Level 3: sponsor-level escalation for business-impacting issues

### Communication cadence
- Weekly stakeholder updates
- Milestone-based status reviews
- Ad hoc updates for issues, risks, or changes in priority
